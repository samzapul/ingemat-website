Hydraulic Engineering Solutions

What Are Amorphous Gabions?

Amorphous gabions are flexible containment units filled with stone or crushed concrete, engineered by INGE&MAT to adapt naturally to the terrain they protect rather than forcing the terrain to conform to a rigid structure.

  • Flexible containment units that conform to irregular surfaces
  • Filled with stone or crushed concrete for mass and permeability
  • Designed to adapt naturally to uneven and dynamic terrain
  • Suitable for both dry installation and underwater placement
  • Purpose-built for hydraulic and marine protection works
