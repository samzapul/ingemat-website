Integrated Coastal Systems

Three product families. One complete engineering system. Each material fulfills a specific hydraulic role.

01

Geosynthetics

Foundation · Separation · Filtration

Nonwoven and woven geotextiles, geogrids and geocells form the invisible foundation of every coastal system — separating materials, controlling drainage and reinforcing weak subgrades.
